Caffeine through the day

Naps Trials pooled

Across 11 studies of 381 people, a daytime nap improved thinking afterwards (effect size 0.18), alertness most (0.29), mainly over the next two hours?. Airline pilots given a planned 40-minute rest on long-haul flights fell asleep in 5.6 minutes, slept about 26, and stayed more alert through descent and landing, with no cost to their sleep later?.

Light and your body clock Lab finding

Light before your body’s low point in the night moves your clock later; light after it, in the morning, moves it earlier; and no long stretch of the day is blind to light?. Ordinary room light before bed delayed melatonin, the body’s night signal, in 99% of people and shortened it by about 90 minutes?.

Burn-out Guideline

The WHO defines it by three dimensions, the first feelings of energy depletion or exhaustion, from chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed?.

Depression Guideline

Its symptoms include feeling very tired or low in energy; an episode lasts most of the day, nearly every day, for at least two weeks. About 5.2% of adults live with it?.

When to ask for help

If you have felt low or drained most days for two weeks or more, talk to a doctor or someone you trust: depression is treatable?. If you think of harming yourself, call your local emergency number now.
